## **Setting:**
### CELEBRATION OF DAWN
This story takes place in a large island country known as [[Nambria]], recognized for its international trade by sea and traditional festivals open for all. Each one of you has signed up to attend its biggest yearly festival, [[Celebration of Dawn]], as guards to make sure everything goes smoothly.... after all, [[the Queen]] is expected to attend and deliver a speech. And receiving **100 gold** pieces each doesn't sound so bad. This year marks the passing of half a millennium
since [[Noctyra]]'s- the goddess of eternal dark's- defeat. bringing with it an age of light and prosperity. [[Celebration of Dawn]] gathers people from all around the
globe in the city of [[Portunis]], located right next to South [[Nambria]]'s largest seaport, to honor their friends and ancestors.
Murmurs rise that this year shall mark the start of a new era.
